Privacy policy

Scrub Rush for iPhone · Effective November 1, 2026

Scrub Rush has no accounts of its own. We collect a small amount of anonymous gameplay and crash data to keep the game working. Optional ads are provided by Google, purchases go through Apple, and live races and Game Center leaderboards go through Apple's Game Center.

Who we are

Scrub Rush is made by Particles of Mind ("we"). Contact: support@particlesofmind.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stars, best scores, the places you opened, your own best runs), your purchases' unlock state and your settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them; purchases can be restored with Restore Purchases in Settings.

What we collect

  • A random player ID. The game makes up a random identifier the first time it starts. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number. You can see it in the game under Settings.
  • Gameplay events. For example: a session started, a place was started or finished with its score and stars, an optional ad was watched, a purchase was made. Each event carries the player ID, the platform (iOS), the app version and the time. We use them to balance the game and find where players get stuck.
  • Leaderboard scores. Your star total and best score per place, stored with the player ID and the racing name you picked from a fixed list in the game (there is no free text), for the game's own leaderboards.
  • Shared ghost runs. Only when you choose to share a run with a friend: we store the run (your machine's positions over the course, score, stars, finish time, the place) with the player ID and your racing name, and give you a six-letter code. Anyone with the code can see the racing name and race the run. Shared runs are deleted after 90 days, and you can delete one sooner in the game. Runs you only race against yourself never leave your phone.
  • Crash reports. If the game crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details (device model, iOS version, app version, what the code was doing) is sent so we can fix it. It contains no personal information.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal content, and we do not sell data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game uses it for live races and Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. Your Game Center player identifier and display name are used by Apple; your scores are sent to Apple's leaderboards; in a live race, your Game Center name, your racing name and your machine's position, score and stars are sent directly to the other player's phone. We do not store any of this on our servers. You can switch Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

Purchases (the no ads subscription, boost colors and costumes) are made through Apple; we never see your payment details. To check what you own and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives the player ID and Apple's record of your purchases.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded ads (for a second chance after a crash, a turbo start or doubled stars) through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one, and not at all with the no ads subscription. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and how you interact with the ad.

  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are less personalized.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown.
  • Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Who processes the data for us

  • Supabase stores the gameplay events, leaderboard scores and shared ghost runs (servers in the United States).
  • Sentry receives crash reports (servers in the United States).
  • RevenueCat checks purchases (servers in the United States).
  • Google AdMob provides the optional ads.
  • Apple provides purchases and Game Center.

How long we keep it

Shared ghost runs are deleted after 90 days. We keep gameplay events, leaderboard scores and crash reports only as long as they help us run and improve the game, and delete them sooner when you ask.

Your choices and rights

To see or delete the data linked to your player ID, including shared ghost runs, email support@particlesofmind.com with the ID (Settings in the game). Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK, Switzerland or California, you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Scrub Rush is rated for ages 9 and up but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; the racing names come from a fixed list and the game has no chat or free text.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
