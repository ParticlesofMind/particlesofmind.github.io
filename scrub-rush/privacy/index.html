Privacy policy

Scrub Rush for iPhone · Effective November 5, 2026

The short version. Scrub Rush has no accounts. You never give us your name, email or phone number to play. The game uses a random player ID, sends gameplay events and crash reports so we can keep it working, and keeps your progress on your phone. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one and come from Google. Apple handles all payments. We do not sell your data.

Who we are

Scrub Rush is published by Benjamin Jack Laubacher, trading as Particles of Mind ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your data. Contact: support@particlesofmind.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (stars, best scores, the places you opened, your own best runs), the awards you have won, your Garage choices (vehicle, paint, costume and boost color), what you have bought, and your settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them. Purchases come back with Restore Purchases in Settings.

If you turn on tilt steering, the game reads your phone's motion sensors on the phone to steer. Nothing from the sensors is sent anywhere.

What we collect

  • A random player ID. The game makes up a random identifier the first time it starts. It is not based on your name, email, Apple Account, phone number or device hardware. You can see it at the bottom of Settings. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Gameplay events. Short records of what happens while you play, for example: a session started, a place was started or finished with its score, stars and time, a goal was reached, an award was won, an optional ad was offered or watched, a purchase was made, a ghost race or live race was started or finished. Each event carries the player ID, the platform (iOS), the app version and the time. We use them to fix problems, balance the game and see where players get stuck.
  • Leaderboard scores. Your star total and best score per place, stored with the player ID and the racing name you picked from a fixed list in the game (there is no free text). Other players see the racing name and the score.
  • Shared ghost runs. Only when you choose to share a run with a friend: we store the run (your machine's positions over the course, score, stars, finish time, the place) with the player ID and your racing name, and give you a six-letter code. Anyone with the code can see the racing name and race the run. Shared runs are deleted after 90 days, and you can delete one sooner in the game. Runs you only race against yourself never leave your phone.
  • Crash reports. If the game crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details (device model, iOS version, app version, what the code was doing) is sent so we can fix it.

When the game talks to these services, they also see the IP address the connection comes from, as any internet service does. We do not use it to identify you.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal content.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game uses it for live races and Game Center leaderboards. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy. Apple uses your Game Center player identifier and display name; your scores go to Apple's leaderboards; in a live race, your Game Center name, your racing name and your machine's position, score and stars go directly to the other player's phone. We do not store any of this on our servers. You can turn Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings.

Purchases

Everything for sale is optional: the No ads subscription, vehicles, paint, costumes and boost colors. Purchases are made through Apple, and we never see your payment details. To check what you own and restore purchases, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives the player ID and Apple's record of your purchases. You cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob: a second chance after a crash, and a turbo start. An ad appears only when you choose to watch one. With the No ads subscription, these offers are free and no ads are shown.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), your IP address and an approximate location worked out from it, device information, and how you interact with the ad. - On iPhone, the game asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are not personalized with it. - In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown. - You can also turn off tracking for all apps in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

How long we keep it

Shared ghost runs are deleted after 90 days. Gameplay events and leaderboard scores are kept only as long as they help us run and improve the game. Crash reports are deleted after the crash service's retention period. If you ask us to delete your data, we delete it.

Your choices and rights

To see, get a copy of, or delete the data linked to your player ID, including shared ghost runs, email support@particlesofmind.com with the ID from Settings. We reply within 30 days. Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK, Switzerland or California, you may also have the right to correct your data, object to how it is used, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority.

We do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking on iPhone, or agree to personalized ads in Google's consent form, Google may use ad data for personalized advertising, which California law can count as "sharing". You can stop it at any time by turning off tracking in iPhone Settings.

Because we only know you by a random player ID, deleting the app also cuts the link between you and the data it sent.

Children

Scrub Rush is rated 9+ but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Racing names come from a fixed list, and the game has no chat or free text. If you think a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Only we can read gameplay events and crash reports; leaderboards show only racing names and scores.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If a change is important, we will also say so in the game.
