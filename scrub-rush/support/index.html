Common questions

How do I play?

Hold the phone sideways. The joystick at the bottom left steers. The buttons at the bottom right boost and honk. Swipe up in the air off a ramp to flip; ride a ramp with only one side's wheels for a side flip.

How do I get three stars?

Three stars means a near perfect run: clean almost every spill, with no crash and nothing bumped. The results card tells you what the next star takes.

How do I race a friend?

Share a ghost run and send your friend the six-letter code, or use Race live to race through Game Center at the same time.

I bought something. How do I get it on a new phone?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel the no ads subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Scrub Rush.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Use Privacy choices in the game's Settings if it is shown. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

Email us with your player ID and we will delete the game data linked to it, including shared ghost runs. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
