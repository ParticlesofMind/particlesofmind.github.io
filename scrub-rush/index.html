Scrub Rush

An arcade cleaning race for iPhone.

Ride a floor scrubber at full speed through the places nobody else wants to clean: an airport after a delay, a stadium after the match, a school cafeteria after a food fight, and 22 more. Scrub every spill, dodge everyone in your way, jump the ramps and flip.

  • 25 places in five zones, each with its own people, sounds and a set piece of its own.
  • Three machines that handle differently: Standard, Speed and Heavy.
  • Race a friend's ghost with a code, or race live through Game Center.

The game is free. Optional ads offer a second chance or a turbo start; you choose when to watch one. A subscription removes ads, and boost colors and driver costumes can be bought.
