Common questions

How do I play?

Hold the phone sideways. Your left thumb moves Luke, your right thumb aims and fires. Sneak up behind a guard for a silent takedown. Sprint and ram charges refill when you clear a floor.

Do I lose my coins when I get thrown out?

No. Coins are kept, so every climb makes Luke stronger for the next one.

Is the game free?

Yes. There are no purchases. Optional ads offer a second parachute or double coins; you decide when to watch one.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Open the game, tap the gear, and use Privacy choices if it is shown. You can also turn off tracking for all apps in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

Email us with your player ID and we will delete the game data linked to it. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.
