Privacy policy

Run Luke, Run! for iPhone · Effective November 1, 2026

Run Luke, Run! has no accounts, no sign-in and no purchases. We collect a small amount of anonymous gameplay and crash data to keep the game working, and the optional ads are provided by Google.

Who we are

Run Luke, Run! is made by Particles of Mind ("we"). Contact: support@particlesofmind.com.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (coins, upgrades, the floors you reached, settings) is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

  • A random player ID. The game makes up a random identifier the first time it starts. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number. You can see it in the game under Settings.
  • Gameplay events. For example: a session started, a floor was started or finished, an optional ad was watched. Each event carries the player ID, the platform (iOS), the app version and the time. We use them to balance the game and find where players get stuck.
  • Best floor. The highest floor you reached, stored with the player ID and the display name "Player", for the game's best-floor ranking.
  • Crash reports. If the game crashes or hits an error, a report with technical details (device model, iOS version, app version, what the code was doing) is sent so we can fix it. It contains no personal information.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal content, and we do not sell data.

Ads

The game shows optional rewarded ads (for a second parachute or double coins) through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and how you interact with the ad.

  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission before the advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still work but are less personalized.
  • In the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown.
  • Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Who processes the data for us

  • Supabase stores the gameplay events and best floors (servers in the United States).
  • Sentry receives crash reports (servers in the United States).
  • Google AdMob provides the optional ads.

How long we keep it

We keep gameplay events, best floors and crash reports only as long as they help us run and improve the game, and delete them sooner when you ask.

Your choices and rights

To see or delete the data linked to your player ID, email support@particlesofmind.com with the ID (Settings in the game). Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK, Switzerland or California, you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Run Luke, Run! is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
