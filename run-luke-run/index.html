Run Luke, Run!

A pixel-art tower climb for iPhone.

Princess Emily is held at the top of a guarded tower. Luke climbs it floor by floor: sneak up behind guards for silent takedowns, or shoot it out. Every guard who beats him throws him out of a window, and he parachutes down to a lively Renaissance village to buy gear, eat, rest and try again.

  • Guards, wolves, drones, wasps, mantises and machines, each with their own senses, strengths and weaknesses.
  • Blasters, cannons and remote traps. Traps buzz anyone who steps on them, Luke included.
  • Secret elevators hidden in lockers and closets.

The game is free. Optional ads offer a second parachute or double coins; you choose when to watch one.
